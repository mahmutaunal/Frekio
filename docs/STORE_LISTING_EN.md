## Full description

```text
Frekio puts live Internet radio within easy reach.

Explore stations from Turkey, search a worldwide directory, save your favorites, and return to recently played stations whenever you want. Frekio stays focused on listening, with a clear interface that works across phones and tablets in light or dark mode.

LISTEN YOUR WAY

• Discover live radio stations from Turkey
• Search stations from around the world
• Save favorites and revisit listening history
• View live track or programme information when a station provides it
• Set a sleep timer for 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes

BUILT FOR BACKGROUND PLAYBACK

Keep listening when the screen is off or while using another app. Control playback from the lock screen, notification panel, Bluetooth accessories, and interactive home-screen widgets. Frekio also supports Android Auto and compatible CarPlay environments for a focused in-car experience.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Frekio requires no account and includes no advertising, analytics, profiling, or tracking SDKs. Favorites, recent stations, and preferences are stored on your device. The project is open source, so its behavior can be inspected and improved by the community.

MODERN AND EFFICIENT

The app uses a responsive interface for Android, iPhone, iPad, and tablets. Station data is cached to reduce unnecessary requests, widget updates are event-driven, and failed streams use a limited retry strategy rather than continuous background polling.

Frekio uses the community-operated Radio Browser directory for public station information. Audio is streamed directly from the broadcaster you select. Frekio does not host, own, or rebroadcast radio content. Station availability, programming, logos, stream quality, and geographic restrictions are controlled by the respective broadcasters.
```
